# Privacy Policy

_Buddy for Zammad – last updated: 23 November 2026_

Buddy for Zammad is a browser extension for agents of the Zammad helpdesk system. It does not collect, transmit,
sell or share any personal data.

## What the extension stores

The following data is stored only locally in your own browser (`storage.local`, not synchronized):

- your settings (enabled features, language),
- the addresses of the Zammad instances you added,
- saved search presets and the history of your last 12 searches,
- the position of moved windows and recently used emojis.

You can delete this data at any time by removing the extension.

## What the extension does not do

- No analytics, tracking or telemetry.
- No network requests of its own; it only works inside the Zammad pages you open.
- No access to websites other than the Zammad instances you grant individually.
- Ticket content is processed only locally in the page (for example to clean pasted text or show the email
  preview) and never leaves your browser through the extension.

## Permissions

- `storage` – saves the settings listed above.
- `scripting` – adds the toolbar and search builder to the Zammad instances you added.
- `activeTab` – lets you add the Zammad instance open in the current tab.
- Host access – requested separately for each Zammad instance you add.
